THE ORIGIN STORY
Why I started SuriLine

Working as a Project and Test Coordinator at Telesur, and wrapping up my Bachelor in Software Engineering, I noticed a recurring friction in daily life in Suriname.

SuriLine was born from a simple frustration: finding businesses, services, or government agencies in Suriname was harder than it needed to be. I wanted to build something that changes that - a platform that makes discovery simple, fast, and accessible for everyone.

I love building products that solve real problems, and SuriLine is the most personal expression of that drive. It is my contribution to modernizing how people connect with the services around them.

THE MISSION

To organize Surinames local information and make it universally accessible and useful. We believe in bridging the gap between physical businesses and digital presence, ensuring no service is ever too hard to find.
