Biography & Background

Driven by solutions, powered by code.

I am a hardworking, motivated software engineering student and product developer based in Suriname. Whether collaborating in multidisciplinary teams or building independently, I focus on solving complex real-world problems and turning daily friction into opportunities for digital innovation.

As the founder of SuriLine, SuriKoers, and Cyberpad Studio, my mission is to modernize digital infrastructure in Suriname and create seamless, accessible platforms for everyday users, local businesses, and creators.

The Origin Story

Why I started SuriLine

The idea for SuriLine sparked from a genuine frustration. Finding reliable, up-to-date information in Suriname—from a local business phone number to the exact location of a government agency or an emergency service—was incredibly fragmented. People relied heavily on Facebook pages, word of mouth, or outdated phone books.

SuriLine was born to build a modern digital guide making local discovery fast, transparent, and accessible for everyone.

The Mission

Digital Accessibility

"To organize Suriname's local information and make it universally accessible and useful, bridging the gap between physical businesses and modern digital presence."
